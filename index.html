Think of Discord as a private group text — except instead of one giant thread, it's organized into rooms by topic. Mentors run the rooms, and stay in control.

Some students are already familiar with Discord and may already use it. Our server is a separate, private space — invite-only, and run by the team's mentors.

  • Discord is not a public social network. Our server is invite-only — only team members, parents, mentors, and alumni are in it.
  • Discord is not a place for strangers. No outside friends, no DMs from people you don't know.
  • Discord is a moderated team workspace. Mentors can see every channel and are responsible for it.
  • Discord is free, on phone or laptop. Notifications can muted per channel — no one needs to be on it 24/7.

There are 5 categories and each category has separate channels. Channels are where the conversations take place. Pick the channel (room) that matches the topic you want to discuss.

The categories are:

  • General: Info and items that are for the whole group
  • Subteams: Each subteam has it's own channel
  • Events: Each event we attend will have it's own channel for conversations and event photos.
  • Restricted: Channels in restricted are limited to particular groups
  • Parents & Alumni: Channels for our parents and alumni to talk with each other

Use the right channel. It's the #1 thing we'll nag you about.

Channel Use it for Who's there
# announcements Official team updates. Mirrors the weekly email. Read-only
# general Team-wide chat that doesn't fit anywhere else. Everyone
# photos Shop shots, build progress, etc... Everyone
# off-topic Memes, music, non-robot chatter. Keep it school-appropriate. Everyone
# cad / electrical / mechanical / etc. Subteam-specific work. Ask questions, share files, coordinate. Everyone
# (competition channels) One per event. All competition photos go here — not in #photos. Everyone
# leads / driveteam / mentors Restricted to specific roles. By role

Your role decides what you see. Mentors have access to every channel — every one. Other roles unlock specific spaces.

Four steps and you're up and running!

  1. Download Discord. App Store, Google Play, or discord.com on a laptop. It's free.
  2. Use the team invite link. A mentor sends you a one-time link. Set your name to your real first & last name.
  3. Mute what you don't need. Right-click any channel → Mute. We recommend keeping #announcements on.
  4. Check in when it suits you. Once a day is plenty. Weekly emails still go out — Discord is a complement, not a replacement.

Now that you are up and running, we'll see you in #General!

Under the general category, you will see a channel called announcements. All announcements will be in this channel, and also emailed to you.

Speak to a mentor, and consider including your parents in the conversation.

  1. Real names only.
    Set your server nickname to your first and last name. No anime handles, no inside jokes — we need to know who's talking.
  2. Team Code of Conduct applies here too.
    Everything you'd say in the shop. Nothing you wouldn't. Gracious professionalism, on the server too.
  3. Use the right channel.
    Subteam questions in subteam channels. Memes in #off-topic. Don't @everyone unless something is on fire.
  4. No 1:1 DMs between students and mentors.
    FIRST Youth Protection Program governs how mentors and students communicate.
    • If a student DMs a mentor, the mentor will redirect the conversation back to a channel. This protects everyone.
    • If a small-group DM is genuinely needed, at least two mentors must be in it, or include your parent. No exceptions.
    • If you need to reach a mentor, do it in a channel.
    • If something feels off, tell a mentor or your team lead immediately. In person, by email, in a channel — whichever feels right. We will take it seriously.
  5. Remember that the mentors are always watching.
    Mentors see every channel. If something feels off — bullying, harassment, anything — tell a mentor.
